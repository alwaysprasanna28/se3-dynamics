title: Robotic Manipulator Dynamics on SE(3)
---

A complete, first-principles course on rigid-body motion, manipulator kinematics, and manipulator dynamics — written in the geometric language of the Lie group $SE(3)$.

> [!abstract] What this course covers
> Rotations and $SO(3)$, rigid motions and $SE(3)$, forward and differential kinematics, single-body and manipulator dynamics, recursive algorithms, operational-space dynamics, and geometric control — ending with worked problems and a formula sheet.
